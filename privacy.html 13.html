Privacy Policy

Daily Hisab – daily income and expense tracker

Effective date: October 2026

Daily Hisab ("the App") is developed by MuhammadSamirKhankabir ("Developer"). This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: the name/username and password or email you enter when you sign up or log in.
  • Financial entries: the income, expense, budget and notes you add inside the App.
  • Settings: your chosen language and other app preferences.

The App does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or device identifiers.

2. How your data is stored

Your entries and settings are stored on your own device, in the App's local storage. The Developer does not receive, view or sell this data. If you clear the App's data or uninstall it, your records may be deleted, so please keep your own backup if needed.

3. How we use information

Your information is used only to run the App's features: login, showing your dashboard, records and budget, and remembering your settings.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone. The App does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your data safe, but no method of storage is completely secure. Please use a strong password and keep your device locked.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries inside the App at any time. To remove all data, clear the App's storage in your device settings or uninstall the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
